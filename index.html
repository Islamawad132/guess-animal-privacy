Guess Animal – Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Guess Animal ("the app") is a simple animal-guessing game for young children, published by Little Explorers. This policy explains what the app does, and does not do, with information.

The short version

The app does not collect, store on our servers, share, or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no in-app purchases, and no connection to any server.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect names, email addresses, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, or any other personal data. The app does not request internet access and does not ask for any device permissions.

Data stored on your device

To remember progress, the app saves a small amount of game data locally on the device only: collected stars and stickers, best score, and preferences such as difficulty level. This data never leaves the device and is deleted when you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

Speech

The "Listen & find" mode reads animal names aloud using the text-to-speech engine already installed on your device. The app does not record audio and does not use the microphone.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking services, and shares no data with third parties.

Children's privacy

The app is designed for children. Because it collects no personal information and has no online features, no data from children is collected, used, or disclosed.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behavior changes in a way that affects privacy, this page will be updated and the date above revised.

Contact
